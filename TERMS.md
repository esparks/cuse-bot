# cuse-bot Terms and Conditions

cuse-bot is a moderation tool for the r/cuse subreddit, run by the r/cuse
moderators.

- It posts schedule information and discussion threads based on public
  information from cuse.com (Syracuse University Athletics).
- Schedule data is provided as-is. Always check cuse.com for official game
  times, venues and results.
- cuse-bot is not affiliated with or endorsed by Syracuse University.
- Use of r/cuse remains subject to Reddit's User Agreement and Content Policy.
- These terms may change at any time. Questions go to the r/cuse moderators
  via modmail.
